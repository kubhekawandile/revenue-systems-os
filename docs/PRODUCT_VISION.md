# Revenue Systems OS — Product Vision

## What it is

Revenue Systems OS is a local-first apprenticeship workspace for Revenue Operations, CRM Architecture, Marketing Operations, Sales Operations, GTM Engineering, and systems thinking. It is a professional practice environment, not an LMS or a general note-taking application.

The product brings the apprenticeship into one organised workspace. Rather than spreading work across disconnected Notion pages, Markdown files, personal notes, and project folders, it provides a consistent structure for curriculum progress, weekly consulting workspaces, portfolio evidence, and future review workflows.

## Why it exists

Revenue systems capability is demonstrated through applied work, judgement, and clear communication—not content consumption alone. Revenue Systems OS is designed around the operating loop:

Learning → Building → Defending → Documenting → Improving → Demonstrating

The workspace makes that loop visible and traceable. A learner can move from a curriculum week into a consulting workspace, record deliverables and decisions, organise supporting evidence, and build a portfolio of work that can later support mentorship and employer-readiness conversations.

## Product principles

### Evidence-based learning

Learning is connected to evidence of practice. The current application provides a structured place for weekly objectives, deliverables, portfolio artifacts, business impact, and completion state. It does not generate or substitute the underlying learning material.

### Portfolio development

Portfolio evidence is organised by phase and week. Each week has an import-ready portfolio entry for consulting artifacts, evidence links, reflection, business impact, communication material, and mentor review. This structure supports a body of demonstrable work rather than a collection of disconnected files.

### Decision journals, mentorship, and review

The product includes placeholders for decision journals and mentor-review information. These are intended to preserve reasoning, feedback, approvals, revision requirements, and lessons learned as the apprenticeship evolves.

### Communication and walkthrough evidence

The platform is designed to organise communication evidence such as executive summaries, stakeholder notes, presentations, walkthroughs, repositories, and supporting files. It does not attempt to replace the specialised tools used to create that work.

### Career and employer readiness

Over time, the workspace should help translate applied work into a clear professional narrative: what was built, why decisions were made, what business outcome was expected, and how the work can be demonstrated to employers or clients.

## Local-first and cost-efficient

Revenue Systems OS runs locally and has no required backend, authentication, API keys, database, or external service dependency. The curriculum and portfolio structures are represented as local application data, keeping the platform inexpensive to run and straightforward to evolve.

External tools remain useful and expected where they are best suited: Miro for boards and diagrams, Loom for walkthroughs, GitHub for repositories, spreadsheets for analysis, CRM tools for system practice, and SQL tools for querying. Revenue Systems OS organises the resulting work and evidence; it does not try to replace every external tool.

## Curriculum source boundary

The authoritative apprenticeship curriculum sources currently available are `Instructions.txt`, `Final Revenue Systems Architecture v4.2`, and the currently generated Week 1 learning material. Weeks 2–30 do not yet have complete generated learning materials.

This product documentation does not duplicate the curriculum. The application currently provides the import-ready structure in which those authoritative sources and future generated materials can be organised.
